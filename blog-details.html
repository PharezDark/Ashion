Being seen: how is age diversity effecting change in fashion and beauty?

  • by Editorial Team
  • Sep 17, 2026
  • 39 Comments

A fashion season can be defined as much by the people on the catwalk as it can by the clothes they are wearing. This time around, a key moment came at the end of Marc Jacobs’ New York show, when an almost makeup-free Christy Turlington made a rare return to the catwalk, aged 50 (she also stars, with the designer himself, in the label’s ad campaign), where the average catwalk model is traditionally much younger.

A few days later, leading designers arguably upped the ante. Runway showcases inspired by timeless icons have increasingly featured models across diverse age spectrums, redefining contemporary standards of elegance and style.

True style transcends generational boundaries. Embracing diversity in fashion broadens our creative horizons and redefines modern luxury.

Shifting cultural landscapes mean that modern consumers look for authentic representation. Brands that embrace true inclusivity are leading the conversation, proving that style is a lifelong journey rather than a fleeting trend.
